If You Smell Gas Right Now

Leave the house immediately without turning any light switches, appliances, or electronics on or off, since a spark from a switch can ignite a real gas leak. Once outside and at a safe distance, call your gas utility's emergency line and then call us. Do not attempt to locate the leak yourself or turn gas valves on or off unless you're specifically trained to do so safely.

New Appliance Installation and Line Extensions

Adding a gas range, a gas water heater, or a gas fireplace insert to a home that didn't previously have that connection means extending or tapping into the existing gas line. The added appliance's demand needs to be sized correctly alongside everything already running on that line. We calculate total gas load before extending any line, since undersizing a shared line causes pressure problems across every appliance connected to it, not just the new one.

Why We Pressure Test Every Gas Line Job

After any gas line repair or installation, we pressure test the line to confirm there are no leaks at any joint or connection before considering the job complete. This isn't optional or an upsell. It's a genuine safety verification step that catches a slow leak at a fitting before it becomes a real hazard, and it's standard practice for any properly performed gas work.

Older Homes and Aging Gas Lines

Some of Delaware's older homes near downtown still have original black iron gas piping that's decades old, which can develop corrosion at threaded joints over time even without an active leak yet. If we're already working on a gas line for another reason, we'll flag any visible corrosion or outdated fittings so you know the real condition of that system, not just the specific issue we came out for.

Gas Appliance Shutoff Valves

Every gas appliance should have its own accessible shutoff valve, which lets you isolate that specific appliance without shutting off gas to the entire house during a repair or in an emergency. Older installations sometimes skip this or have a valve that's seized from disuse, and we add or replace individual shutoffs as part of most gas line work since it's a meaningful safety improvement for a relatively small added cost.
