Septic and Well Service Is a Real, Everyday Part of Ostrander Plumbing

Ostrander was laid out in 1852 and incorporated in 1875, and it remains a genuinely small, rural village with just over a thousand residents according to the 2020 census, inside Scioto Township, a mostly agricultural township of about 35 square miles. Outside the village's small footprint, most surrounding Scioto Township properties rely on private septic systems rather than a municipal sewer connection, and many rely on private wells rather than a city water line.

That combination means septic tank pumping and inspection is a genuinely routine call here, not a rare exception. Well water's natural mineral content can also differ noticeably from municipal supply, which is worth factoring in before assuming a fixture problem is the same as it would be on city water.

Ostrander's Older Village Core

The village's own small footprint includes real 19th-century commercial buildings along Main and North Street, near the site of the old Farmers Exchange that once served the CCC&I Railroad. The towering 1948 grain elevator still visible from a distance is a genuine landmark for the area. Homes in this older village core are a realistic age for original supply lines needing evaluation, the same as older housing anywhere else in the county.

What a Rural Service Call Actually Involves

A rural Ostrander or Scioto Township property often means a longer driveway, a septic system rather than a city sewer cleanout, and sometimes a private well pressure tank as part of the whole picture. We ask about these specifics when scheduling a rural call so the technician arrives prepared for the property's actual setup rather than assuming a standard in-town connection.

Emergency Response Still Applies to Rural Addresses

A rural location doesn't mean a slower response. Our line is answered 24 hours a day for Ostrander and the rest of Scioto Township the same as anywhere else in Delaware County, and we dispatch based on the actual emergency, not the address type. A burst pipe or a failing sump pump matters just as much on a rural property as it does inside the village.

Agricultural Properties and Outbuildings

Scioto Township's genuinely agricultural character means some properties we service include a farmhouse plus outbuildings with their own separate water or drain connections, which is a different scope than a standard single-structure residential call. We ask about the full property layout when scheduling so we bring the right equipment and understand which structures are actually connected to which system before we arrive.
