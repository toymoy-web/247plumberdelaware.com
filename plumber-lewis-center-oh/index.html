Winter Freeze Risk Along the Olentangy Corridor

Lewis Center sits on the east side of the Olentangy River, directly across from Powell, and its real hard-freeze winters put the same exterior-wall and crawlspace supply lines at risk as anywhere else in Delaware County. Newer homes in developments like the Village at Alum Creek, many built in the 2000s under the Olentangy Local School District, still have exterior hose bibs and garage-run lines that freeze if not properly protected before a hard freeze.

Basements near Alum Creek State Park and the Olentangy floodplain see real groundwater pressure during spring thaw, which is worth a sump pump check before storm season rather than after a basement has already taken on water.

What to Check Before You Call

If your water heater is leaking or making unusual noises, note whether the leak is from the tank body itself or a visible connection, since that distinction speeds up an accurate diagnosis. For a basement near Alum Creek or the Olentangy floodplain, check whether your sump pump is cycling normally before a forecasted heavy rain, rather than waiting to find out during the storm itself.
