Why a Line That Keeps Clogging Needs More Than a Snake

A drain snake works by punching a hole through a blockage, which restores flow immediately but leaves buildup coating the rest of the pipe's interior. "Hydro jetting" uses a high-pressure water stream, run through a specialized nozzle, to scour the entire interior wall of the pipe clean rather than just clearing a path through the center. That's the real reason a jetted line often goes far longer before clogging again compared to one that's only ever been snaked.

It's the right call specifically for a line with a repeat clogging pattern, tree root intrusion, or years of grease buildup, rather than a first-time, isolated clog, which a standard snake usually resolves on its own.

What Hydro Jetting Handles That Snaking Doesn't

Grease buildup that has hardened along a kitchen line's interior over years responds far better to jetting than to a snake, since the high-pressure water actually breaks down and flushes the coating rather than pushing through its center. Root intrusion in an older clay tile sewer lateral, common in Delaware's tree-lined older neighborhoods, gets cut and flushed out by the jetting nozzle in a way a snake's cable typically cannot match.

What We Check Before Recommending It

Jetting uses meaningful water pressure, so we camera-inspect the line first on any pipe with unknown condition or age, since a severely deteriorated or already-cracked pipe can be damaged further by jetting rather than helped by it. For a line in reasonable structural condition with a buildup or root problem, jetting is both safe and the more durable fix than repeated snaking.

How Often a Jetted Line Needs a Repeat Visit

A line jetted purely for grease buildup, with no structural root intrusion, often stays clear for years afterward, since the underlying cause was simply removed rather than an ongoing structural issue. A line with active root intrusion at a joint will eventually regrow roots into that same opening unless the joint itself gets a spot repair or trenchless lining. That's why we're upfront about jetting being maintenance rather than a permanent fix in that specific situation.

Commercial and Multi-Unit Lines

Restaurants and other food-service properties in Delaware generate far more grease volume than a typical home, and their lines often need jetting on a set schedule rather than reactively. A backup during business hours is a real operational and health-code problem, not just an inconvenience. We work with commercial property managers to set a recurring maintenance interval based on the actual grease volume a specific kitchen produces, rather than a generic annual default.

What to Do Before We Arrive

Keep pets and children away from the work area while equipment and hoses are staged, both in the house and near any outdoor cleanout access, since the equipment involves real water pressure and a running truck-mounted unit. We'll walk you through where access is needed before starting so you know what areas to keep clear for the visit.
