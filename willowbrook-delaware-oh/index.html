About Willowbrook

Willowbrook is a 213 home neighborhood in Delaware, Ohio, built in three phases. The first families moved into Phase 1 in 2017, and the community was finished in 2024.

The homeowners association maintains nearly two acres of shared common area across two spaces and runs a design review process for exterior projects like fences, decks and additions.

Homes in Their First Years

Every Willowbrook home is less than a decade old, and the newest are only a couple of years old. At this stage a plumbing call is rarely about worn pipe. It is usually a fixture, a valve, an appliance or an installation detail.

A drip under a sink, a toilet that keeps running or a water heater that will not hold temperature are the kinds of problems we expect in newer houses. See toilet repair and faucet and sink repair.

Water Treatment and Water Heaters

Builders usually do not install a softener or filter, so many owners add one after moving in. The City of Delaware's 2022 water quality report lists hardness at 121 ppm. If your water comes from the City, a test at your own tap helps you decide. We cover water filtration and water softener work.

If the water heater is not keeping up, we check it before suggesting a replacement. Our water heater repair page lists what we look at first.

Exterior Projects, the HOA and 811

Because the association reviews exterior projects, tell us early if a plumbing job touches the outside of the house, such as a new hose bib, a softener drain or a sump discharge. We describe the scope so you can submit it for approval.

If you are planning a deck or a fence, call 811 before any digging so buried lines are marked. It is a free call and it prevents expensive damage.

Warranties, Winter and Emergencies

If a leak or failure is covered by your builder warranty, contact the builder about it, and tell us what we find so you can share it. Disconnect outdoor hoses before a hard freeze, and learn where your main shutoff is.

For a burst pipe or active leak, close the main valve and call (513) 796-9381. The line is answered 24/7. See burst pipe repair for the first steps.
