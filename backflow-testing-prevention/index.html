What Backflow Prevention Actually Protects Against

A backflow prevention device stops water from flowing backward into the public water supply, which matters because a drop in main line pressure can otherwise pull contaminated water from an irrigation system, a commercial process line, or a chemical feed system back into drinking water. "Backflow testing and prevention" covers testing an existing device annually, as most water providers and local code require, and installing or repairing the device itself when needed.

Who Actually Needs This in Delaware

Any property with an irrigation or sprinkler system connected to the water supply needs a backflow prevention device, since fertilizer and soil bacteria in irrigation lines are a real contamination risk if backflow occurs. Commercial properties with certain process connections, boiler feed lines, or fire suppression systems also typically require testing under local code. A straightforward residential property with no irrigation system and standard fixtures usually doesn't need a dedicated device.

What Testing Actually Involves

Testing checks whether the device's internal check valves are holding pressure correctly and preventing reverse flow, using calibrated test equipment connected to the device's test ports. A passing device gets documented for your records and, where required, submitted to the local water provider. A failing device gets repaired or replaced, then retested to confirm it passes before we consider the job done.

Staying on Top of Your Annual Requirement

Missing an annual testing deadline can put a property out of compliance with local water provider requirements, which is worth avoiding since catching up after a lapse sometimes involves extra paperwork or scrutiny. We can set up a standing annual reminder for your property so testing happens on schedule without you needing to track the date yourself.

Device Placement and Winterization

An outdoor backflow device, common on irrigation systems, needs proper winterization before Delaware's hard freezes arrive, since a device that freezes and cracks needs full replacement rather than simple repair. We can handle winterization at the same visit as your annual test, timing it to close out the irrigation season before the first real cold snap.
